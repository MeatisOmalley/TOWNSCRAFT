# V3 edit log: sizes and compaction pause (2026-10-14)

V2 media reserved 384 pairs of 12 KiB edit slots (24 KiB per pair, 9 MiB),
so only 384 of 4,096 columns (9%) could ever be edited. Full V2 capacity would
have reserved 96 MiB. V3 keeps V2's immutable bytes and replaces the pool with
a preallocated log of sector-aligned RLE records, placed by the game. The
contract is in [LARGE_WORLD_STORAGE.md](../LARGE_WORLD_STORAGE.md#v3-edit-log-2026-10-14);
guest smoke reports are in [the JSON](edit-log-v3-2026-10-14.json).

## File sizes (seed 4242, crown-leaf bake)

| Medium | Log | `TERRAIN.TMP` | Disk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| V2, 384 pairs (current players) | 9 MiB pool, 384 columns | 33,835,008 | — |
| V3, 486 media (1990 and 1992 profiles) | 49,152 sectors (24 MiB) | 49,563,648 | 206,438,400 |
| V3, 386 40 MB IDE disk | 30,668 sectors (15.0 MiB) | 40,099,840 | 42,519,040 |

The immutable prefix is 47,652 sectors (24.4 MB). On the 386 the log takes
what the FAT16 volume (20,708 clusters of 2 KiB) holds beside `WORLD.SAV`,
less 1 MiB of headroom for small files.

Every base column, edited once, needs 28,343 sectors (13.8 MiB): records
average 6.92 sectors (3,288 bytes; 5 to 11 sectors). The 386 log fits that
with 2,325 sectors (1.1 MiB) to spare; the 486 log with 20,809 (10.2 MiB).
The spare space holds records only an older save still references, and play
keeps 624 sectors in reserve for the next save's flush. With the whole world
edited, the 386 disk therefore has about 1,700 sectors (some 240 records) for
changes between saves; a column changed in two successive save intervals
holds three records. Past that, play asks for a save (see Limits).

## What the native tests ran

`tests/test_edit_log.py` drives the real world cache, V5 save codec and DOS
file adapters (`tests/edit_log_native.c`), one process per step. Synthetic
base columns follow the seed's size histogram (28,544 sectors for 4,096
columns, against the seed's 28,343).

- All 4,096 columns edited, saved every 512 and reloaded, on both log
  sizes: 0 refused records; 2,124 sectors left on the 386 size, 20,608 on
  the 486 size. Both banks verify (4,096 and 3,584 edited records).
- Wraparound: 64 columns rewritten 30 times on an 8,192-sector log (1,920
  writes): the head wrapped and reused released records; nothing refused.
- Compaction on a nearly full 2,048-sector log: 6 compactions moved 35
  records (243 sectors); all data reloads after the next commits.
- A crash between record writes and the bank commit, a damaged newer bank,
  corrupt records and overlapping or out-of-log directory entries.
- A populated 384-pair V2 disk (both banks, 484 records, 100 columns differ
  between banks) migrates through `tools/log_player_vm.py` in 3.9 to 4.3 s,
  loads from either bank, and then accepts edits past the old 384 limit.
  Dry runs on the three real player disks (none has a committed save yet)
  took 5.0 to 6.5 s each.

## Compaction pause

A save compacts only when fewer than 52 free 24-sector runs remain. It then
copies the live records of one window, 1/16 of the log, so one save moves at
most 3,072 sectors (486 media) or 1,917 sectors (386 disk), plus one record
that straddles the window edge.

Guest measurements, 1990 DX/40 profile (486DX/40, ST4385N SCSI timing,
absolute INT 25h/26h sector I/O), from the new-world smoke:

| Work | 100 Hz ticks | Per item |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 64 copies of a 7-sector record (read, checksum, write, DOS commit) | 21 | 3.3 ms (0.47 ms per sector) |
| 64 exact bitmap rebuilds (24 MiB log) | 49 | 7.7 ms |
| 64 full free-run counts, fragmented log (7 used, 3 free; 9,830 runs) | 113 | 17.7 ms |
| 64 failing 24-sector searches, same bitmap | 91 | 14.2 ms |
| Forced compaction at the smoke's save (1 record, 7 sectors) | 2 | 20 ms |

An earlier run of the same probe measured 17 ticks for the copies; ticks are
10 ms, so these are coarse. Upper bound on a 486DX/40: a full window is
3,072 x 0.47 ms = 1.4 s of copying (0.9 s for the 386 disk's window), plus
two rebuilds and one window scan, about 1.5 s in all. This applies only to saves made when the log is nearly full. The
386SX-25 itself was not measured; its CPU-bound parts scale with clock and
architecture, and its IDE transfer cost was not measured.

A dirty play-time eviction also searches the bitmap and counts free runs
until it has found the 26 it needs, so on a nearly full, fragmented log it
costs at most about 30 ms on this machine; on an emptier log it stops early.
An earlier build scanned bit by bit and took 43 ms per count over an
alternating bitmap; the scans now jump between run boundaries (BSF).

## Limits

- Play-time records (ordinary eviction of an edited column) must leave 26
  free 24-sector runs, enough for a save to flush all 25 resident columns.
  Below that the column stays resident and the game shows "Terrain backing
  store full" until the player saves; the save may use the reserve, and its
  commit releases records only the overwritten bank referenced.
- Static RAM grows by 40 KiB (8 KiB bitmap, 32 KiB for both banks' records).
  The 4 MiB 386 profile was not re-run with this build.
- Records stay sector aligned: packing two records into one sector would let
  a torn write damage a record a committed bank still needs.
- Leaf-metadata migrations (`leaf_flags_media`, `crown_leaves_media`) refuse
  V3 media for now.
